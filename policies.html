Policies

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Editorial standards and corrections

I report what I can verify, name my sources where I can, and keep reporting separate from opinion. Anything marked “My take” is my own opinion.

The headlines under “What I’m following” are collected automatically from the outlets named on them, and each one links to the original. I do not write or edit them.

If you find an error in my work, email kelvinkamauk2@gmail.com with the link. I will check it, correct it, and say what changed.

Sponsored content and client work

Any paid placement or sponsored content on this site is labelled “Sponsored”. Sponsors and clients do not control my reporting. When I report on a subject that I also do paid work for, I say so.

Privacy

Newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address is stored by MailerLite, the service that sends the newsletter. I use it only to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Donations. Tips are handled by M-Pesa or PayPal. I never see your card details.

This website. It does not run advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which can see that a page was requested.

Contacting me. If you email or WhatsApp me, I keep your message only to reply and to do the work you ask for.

Your choices. To be removed from the newsletter, use the unsubscribe link. To ask me to delete anything else I hold about you, email kelvinkamauk2@gmail.com.
